CampusNav is not a static demonstration. It runs live mathematical graph algorithms on authentic spatial data.

Dual Routing Engine

The engine automatically picks A* Search with a Haversine heuristic for point-to-point trips and Dijkstra's Algorithm when searching for the nearest of several places. Optimize by shortest distance or walking time.

Accessibility-Aware

Guaranteed wheelchair accessibility. The routing graph actively filters out stairs, steep ascents, and unpaved pathways when accessibility mode is enabled.

Dynamic Detours & Blockages

When campus pathways close due to construction or events, administrators update the live database. The routing engine instantly reroutes pedestrian traffic.
